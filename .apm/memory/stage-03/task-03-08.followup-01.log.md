# Task 3.8 - Follow-up 01: Keep the terminal feel on Back and Forward, and keep the box from shrinking

Original log: `.apm/memory/stage-03/task-03-08.log.md`. No earlier follow-up.

## Summary
Both fixes are in. Back, Forward and a typed address now replace the old content at once, show the destination's prompt at once at full opacity, and type the command (`tree` or `cat <name>`) an interval later, with no `cd` and no clear. A direct load plays the landing, then does the same. During a click sequence the terminal box keeps its pre-click height, to the pixel, from the start of the fade-out until the new prompt appears; a page that was scrolled goes to the top when the old content disappears, and nothing moves after the new prompt. One behaviour differs from the Task's wording: the box is held with a fixed `height`, not a `min-height`, because a minimum cannot stop a taller destination from growing the box (the measurement showed it growing from 142px to 286px and 388px, and from 320px to 388px).

## Important Findings
- **`height`, not `min-height`.** The Task said a minimum height. A minimum cannot hold the box when the destination is taller: the rows are in the layout from the swap (hidden, at opacity 0), so the box grew by up to 246px (for example the root page after a file page: 142px to 388px). I used `height`, which holds exactly. While it is held, content taller than the box overflows it, and that content is hidden (opacity 0). One side effect: invisible rows that overflow the box can still be clicked for those 1 to 4 seconds. They were there before, as hidden rows.
- **At the release the box jumps to its natural height at once**, up or down (for example 423px to 320px at the new prompt after a landing click). That is the "follows its content again" the Task asked for, and it is not animated. It is a visible step in the box's bottom edge. The top does not move.
- **The scroll jumps to the top once**, at the end of the fade-out, for a page scrolled down. I did not animate it.
- **Back and Forward in minimal mode** still show the `cd` line (at once). Only the animated mode drops it, as the Task said.
- **The typed `cd nope/` on the error page** is typed an interval after its prompt, a small change I made for consistency with the other commands.
- `tools/` is untracked in the root checkout, as before.
