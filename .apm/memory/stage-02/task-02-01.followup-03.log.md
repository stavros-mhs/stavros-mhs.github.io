## Important Findings
1. **The `.landing` offset is a desktop-only margin the Task did not ask for.** The Task's rule (`min-height: 100vh`, `padding: 72px 0`) on its own centres the terminal 104px low, at +103.99px at every desktop height. The landing starts at y = 104, below the navbar's 72px height and its 32px bottom margin. I added `margin-top: -104px` on desktop, so the landing starts at the top of the viewport. The page was also 32px taller than the viewport, because the body's 32px bottom padding sits below the landing. I added `margin-bottom: -32px` on desktop to take that back. Phones have neither margin, so their layout is unchanged. The `-104px` is tied to the navbar's current 72px height, so a taller navbar would move the terminal, as the 28px and 32px previews show.
2. **The 28px and 32px names do not fit at 601px.** At 601px the navbar has 441px to the left of the toggle. The 28px names need 493px and the 32px names need 551px. Their row can't shrink, so the toggle is pushed right and off the window's edge. The 24px names do fit. The User's choice between sizes should take this into account, and the choice may need a narrower breakpoint or wrapping (not chosen here).
3. **Spacing at 1280px is wide.** With the names spread across the full width, the gaps are about 248px at 1280px and 301px at 1440px. In the screenshot the row reads as spread across the bar, as the Task asks, but the names look far apart. The spacing was not changed, as the Task says. The User may want a smaller spread, such as a maximum gap or a centred group.
4. **Commit history.** My earlier commit `f8b87a8` had a stale comment, and I amended it, giving `597354d`. Then I added the desktop bottom margin and amended again, giving `a3ec74b`. The branch is local and unpushed, and this commit replaced no reviewed commit. The Task's single commit is kept.
5. **Hover, focus and colours are unchanged.** Only the layout of the names and the landing's box changed. The focus ring, the hover colour and the link colours are the same rules as before.
6. **Commit trailers.** None, as `AGENTS.md` requires. This follows Task 1.2's Important Finding 6.
