## Important Findings
**Open questions for the User. Measured facts only. I did not decide any of them.**

**(a) Light-mode footnote contrast.** The `> cd ..` footnote uses `--dir` `#b5602e` on the page background `#f4ecdc`: 3.82:1 at rest, 5.64:1 on hover (`--dir-hover` `#8f4a1f`). It is normal text, 4.5:1 is needed, and the Spec's exception covers directory names on the terminal surface (3.31:1 there), not the footnote. In dark mode it is 6.90:1 at rest and 9.26:1 on hover. The same pair, `--dir` on the page background in light mode, is also used by the navbar names and the phone panel names, so the same ratio applies there: 3.82:1. That passes 3:1 for the 24px desktop names, as large text, and fails 4.5:1 below 24px (see d).

**(b) Dark prompt against the directory colour.** `--prompt` `#edae77` and `--dir` `#edab71`: contrast between them 1.02:1, CIE76 colour difference 2.49. On the surface `#4a4a4a`, the prompt is 4.61:1 and the directory colour is 4.50:1 (4.5006 unrounded). In light mode they differ clearly: `#934e25` and `#b5602e`, 1.39:1 between them, colour difference 12.22.

**(c) Animations switch over the footnote at 360px.** The switch is fixed at the bottom right, 220px wide and 43px tall (`left 132, right 352` at 360px). On `#/user/WHOAMI.md` the footnote (84px wide, 26px tall, `left 260, right 344`) sits under it when the page is at scroll 0:
- 360x844, minimal and animated: overlap 84 by 23 px (footnote `top 813`, switch `top 793`).
- 360x780, animated: overlap 84 by 21 px, after the page scrolled itself to 62. In minimal mode at 360x780 the footnote is below the fold (`top 813`) and does not overlap.
- 390x780, minimal and animated: overlap 84 by 20 and 21 px. This is new: earlier logs said 390px did not overlap, but only 390x844 was measured. At 390x844 there is no overlap (footnote `top 752`, switch `top 793`).
- Scrolled to the end, there is no overlap in any case. The page is scrollable (`scrollHeight` 930 at 360px and 869 at 390px). In `phone-check.mjs`, also at 360x780 the markdown text itself (`I'm passionate about too...`) is under the switch at scroll 0 in minimal mode, which is the same effect with text and not only the footnote.
- So it depends on the viewport height. It occurs whenever the footnote falls in the switch's band at the start. A fix needs a change of spacing or of the switch's place, which changes the footnote's or the switch's design, so I did not attempt it.
